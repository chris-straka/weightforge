## Left as they are (smells), and why

- `solve.rs:180` the CG result is used without checking it converged (4000
  iterations). On these meshes (under 20k vertices) patches converge; a
  warning would be the next step if a 300k mesh ever comes through.
- `transfer.rs:118` ring search visits the whole cube per ring (O(r^4)) and
  `TriIndex::new` registers a large triangle in every cell of its box.
  Performance only; not hit on the cast.
- `fix.rs:132-151, 226-239` smoothing passes grow with density squared,
  capped at 2000, cloning every vertex's list per pass. Performance only.
- `fix.rs:563` `smooth(.., 0, 0.5)` runs zero passes (dead). Turning it on
  changes the joint-band candidate; it is an idea to measure, not a fix.
- `pick.rs:125` `better` is not transitive within 1e-9: harmless.
- Comments that overclaim, now noted here: the module doc's "never worse
  than the input in any region" (the guarantee is overall: pass, then
  score, and no new failing finding); "only active vertices change" (the
  seam blend reaches 1.2 feature sizes past the active area).

## Judged by what is seen, not the score

rule the benchmark (fix's own objective) was fixed first:

  each region that still tears takes the candidate that tears least there,

- With the guard on, the score's final gate and the with/without-external
  comparison judge by torn skin (cm2) instead of the score.
- Candidates' joints map by structural role when names differ (Tripo's
  Mixamo rig onto the standard skeleton).

1.05 -> 0.67% on the poses. Lysan: no region where Tripo tears less; stays

1.57%, Maeva (coat off) 3.46% (its forearms follow the thighs, up to 43 cm),
so its body loses and its crotch wins: it stays as a candidate for the thighs.
